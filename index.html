Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 2026

FitnessGraph respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information FitnessGraph uses, how information is stored, how long it is retained, how users can delete their information, and how third-party services used by the app may process data.

Information We Use

FitnessGraph may use information that you provide or that is generated while using the app, including:

  • Daily step count
  • Daily calorie intake entered by the user
  • Daily step goal
  • Weight and profile information entered by the user
  • Application preferences and settings

Your personal fitness records and profile information are stored locally on your device. FitnessGraph does not require you to create an online user account.

Firebase

FitnessGraph uses Firebase Realtime Database to download updated food and nutrition database information used by the food calorie search feature.

FitnessGraph does not use Firebase Realtime Database to upload or store your personal step records, calorie intake records, weight, or profile information.

Advertising and Third-Party Services

FitnessGraph uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. Google and its advertising technologies may process information such as device identifiers, advertising identifiers, IP address, app interactions, and diagnostic information for purposes such as providing, measuring, securing, and improving advertising services, subject to Google's own policies and user consent or device settings where applicable.

FitnessGraph does not sell your personal fitness records. Information processed independently by Google AdMob is subject to Google's applicable privacy policies and terms.

Permissions

FitnessGraph may request device permissions necessary to provide its features, including:

  • Physical Activity / Activity Recognition: Used to access step-count information and provide daily step tracking.
  • Foreground Health Service: Used to support ongoing step tracking while the app is running in the background. A foreground service notification may be displayed while this service is active.
  • Notifications: Used where supported and permitted to display notifications related to ongoing step tracking.
  • Internet: Used to download food database updates and load advertisements.
  • Network State: Used to determine network availability for online functionality.

Data Sharing

FitnessGraph does not sell or rent your personal fitness records. Your locally stored step, calorie intake, weight, goal, and profile information is not uploaded by FitnessGraph to its Firebase food database.

Third-party services integrated into the app, such as Google AdMob, may independently process certain device, advertising, usage, or diagnostic information as described in the Advertising and Third-Party Services section above.

Data Retention

FitnessGraph stores your personal fitness records, profile information, step goals, calorie intake information, weight information, and application preferences locally on your device.

This locally stored information is generally retained for as long as the application and its application data remain on your device, unless you remove or reset the information earlier using available app or Android device controls.

FitnessGraph does not maintain user accounts and does not retain your personal fitness records on its own remote servers.

Information processed independently by third-party services such as Google AdMob may be retained according to the applicable third-party provider's own retention policies.

Data Deletion

FitnessGraph does not maintain online user accounts or store your personal fitness records on its own remote servers. Therefore, there is no FitnessGraph server-side personal fitness account data that requires an account deletion request.

You can delete locally stored FitnessGraph information from your device by clearing the app's storage/data through Android settings. Uninstalling FitnessGraph also removes the app's locally stored information from the device, subject to Android's device backup and restore settings where applicable.

If you have a privacy or data-related question, or believe that information associated with you requires further assistance, you can contact us using the email address provided in the Contact section below.

Data Security

FitnessGraph uses reasonable measures designed to protect application data. Personal fitness records handled directly by FitnessGraph are primarily stored locally on your device.

No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure, and the security of locally stored data also depends on the security of your device.

Children's Privacy

FitnessGraph is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly design the app to collect personal information from children under 13.

If you believe a child has provided information through the app in a manner that requires our attention, please contact us using the email address below.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to FitnessGraph, applicable requirements, or third-party services used by the app.

Any updated Privacy Policy will be published on this page with an updated effective date when appropriate.

About FitnessGraph

FitnessGraph is a fitness tracking application designed to help users monitor daily steps, track calorie intake, set activity goals, view fitness history, and monitor their fitness progress.
