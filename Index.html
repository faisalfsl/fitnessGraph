Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 2026

FitnessGraph respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app uses, how it is stored, and how it is protected.

Information We Collect

  • Daily step count
  • Daily calorie intake entered by the user
  • Daily step goal
  • Application preferences

All fitness information is stored locally on your device.

Firebase

FitnessGraph uses Firebase Realtime Database only to download updated food nutrition data. No personal information is uploaded to Firebase.

Advertising

FitnessGraph uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. Google may collect advertising identifiers and anonymous diagnostic information according to Google's Privacy Policy.

Permissions

  • Physical Activity Permission (Step Counter)
  • Internet Permission
  • Network State Permission

Data Sharing

FitnessGraph does not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties.

Data Security

Your data is stored locally on your device. Reasonable security measures are used to protect application data.

Children's Privacy

FitnessGraph is not intended for children under the age of 13.

Changes to this Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any future updates will appear on this page.

About FitnessGraph

FitnessGraph is a fitness tracking application designed to help users monitor daily steps, track calorie intake, and maintain a healthier lifestyle.
